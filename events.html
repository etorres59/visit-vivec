Events & Attractions

Discover the wonders of Vivec City - from grand festivals to sacred pilgrimages, experience the heart of Dunmer culture and divine heritage.

🌟 Festival of Lord Vivec - Special Summer Event

Join thousands of pilgrims for the grandest celebration of the year! Witness divine performances, sacred rituals, and receive blessings from the Temple priests.

🏛️

Temple Pilgrimage Tours

Daily • Dawn to Dusk

Experience the spiritual heart of Vivec City with guided tours through the sacred Temple Canton. Visit the Palace of Vivec and witness the floating Ministry of Truth.

⚔️

Arena Championships

Weekly Tournaments • Every Sundas

Witness epic battles in Vivec's legendary Arena! Watch warriors from across Morrowind compete for glory, gold, and the title of Grand Champion.

🎭

Cultural Arts Festival

Monthly • First Loredas

Immerse yourself in Dunmer arts and culture! Enjoy traditional music, poetry readings of the 36 Lessons, and exhibitions of Dwemer artifacts.

🌊

Waterfront Markets

Daily • Merchants arrive at sunrise

Browse exotic goods from across Tamriel at the bustling Foreign Quarter waterfront. Find rare spices, enchanted items, and treasures from distant lands.

🔮

Mages Guild Lectures

Bi-weekly • Middas Evenings

Attend fascinating lectures on mysticism, enchantment, and the mysteries of Dwemer technology. Open to all seekers of knowledge and magical arts.

🍷

Hlaalu Wine Tastings

Every Fredas • Evening

Sample the finest vintages from across Morrowind at elegant tastings hosted by House Hlaalu. Pair exquisite wines with traditional Dunmer cuisine.

Must-See Attractions

🏰

Palace of Vivec

The divine residence of Lord Vivec himself. Marvel at the floating architecture and sacred chambers where the living god holds court with mortals and immortals alike.

🌙

Ministry of Truth

Witness the awe-inspiring sight of Baar Dau, the "rogue moon" suspended above the Temple Canton by Vivec's divine will. A testament to divine power and protection.

🏛️

High Fane Temple

The spiritual center of the Tribunal Temple. Participate in sacred ceremonies, receive blessings from Ordinators, and study the teachings of the Three.

⚒️

Redoran Canton

Explore the warrior-house district with its imposing crab-shell architecture. Visit training grounds, weapon smiths, and the proud halls of House Redoran.

💰

Hlaalu Canton

The commercial heart of Vivec City. Browse luxury shops, negotiate with merchant princes, and experience the wealth and influence of House Hlaalu.

🧙‍♂️

Telvanni Canton

Enter the mysterious domain of the wizard-lords. See impossible magical architecture and perhaps catch a glimpse of the eccentric but powerful Telvanni masters.

🌍

Foreign Quarter

A cosmopolitan district where outlanders gather. Experience diverse cultures, exotic foods, and goods from across the Empire in this melting pot of Tamriel.

⚔️

The Grand Arena

Test your skills or watch legendary battles in Morrowind's premier fighting venue. From novice duels to championship matches that draw crowds from across the province.
